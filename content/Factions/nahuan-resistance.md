# Nahuan Resistance

> [!abstract] Overview
> The Nahuan Resistance is an underground partisan rebel group that was secretly established to protect the majority-Nahuan migrant population in Three Rivers [22, 175]. Labeled as terrorists by the elvish Imperium, they operated in the shadows to disrupt Imperial oppression and cartel extortion [22, 127].

## Description & Atmosphere
The resistance is a highly decentralized, scrappy network of refugees, thieves, and local partisans [21, 22]. They operate out of hidden cellars, back alleys, and warehouse basements in the Nahuan Quarter, using hit-and-run tactics and sabotage to fight back against discriminatory laws and militia violence [22, 23].

## Key Inhabitants & Associated NPCs
- [[Arcturus Erevan]]: The half-elf diplomat who secretly organized, funded, and equipped the movement [22, 121].
- [[Lilith Erevan]]: An undercover Sisterhood assassin who met and married Arcturus while gathering intelligence on the movement [76].
- [[Theodemar]]: A wealthy high elf noble and cat burglar who supported their cause and harbored transients at his estate [161, 163].

## Points of Interest / Sub-Factions
- [[The Docks Cell]]: A specialized squad focused on monitoring and sabotaging cartel shipments entering the Three Rivers harbor [118, 123].
- [[The Nahuan Quarter Network]]: Safe houses and communication lines nested within the migrant neighborhood [23].

## Campaign History & Session Events
- **Session 1-3**: Under Arcturus's guidance, the resistance targeted Imperial assets and cartel shipments, culminating in the destruction of a major cartel barge [22, 118, 123].
- **Session 4**: In direct retaliation for the resistance's harbor raid, an Imperial-backed extremist pure-blood militia launched a devastating pogrom, burning the Nahuan Quarter to the ground and killing hundreds [23, 127]. This catastrophic event dismantled the resistance's base of operations, scattering its leaders and forcing Arcturus into exile in the desert [23].
