# Ironclaw Cartel

> [!abstract] Overview
> The Ironclaw Cartel is a brutal, highly organized criminal syndicate that dominates the Nahuan desert, extorting local towns and digging up ancient magical relics [114, 132, 149]. Their operations are heavily funded by widespread corruption, having successfully bribed numerous Nahuan government officials [18, 118].

## Description & Atmosphere
In cities under their complete control, such as Mont Rey, the cartel enforces rigid order, maintaining clean, well-kept streets and eliminating petty crime through extreme fear and intimidation [132, 138]. They operate out in the open, displaying immense wealth and using corrupt city officials, double agents, and mercenary forces to maintain their de facto regime [118, 132].

## Key Inhabitants & Associated NPCs
- [[Huitecoc]]: A terrifying half-orc warlock "Tracker" who serves Eternata Aelara by hunting down the last of the Jaguar guardians [55, 131].
- [[Xotolin Swiftclaw]]: A ferocious Tabaxi berserker who managed dock defenses in Three Rivers [121, 168].
- [[Mateo]]: A double-agent merchant in Mont Rey who reports suspicious travelers to cartel leaders [133, 140].
- [[Jesus Maria "Chema" Gonzales]]: A treacherous agent who sets up alleyway ambushes for the syndicate [145].
- [[Teagan Kearie]] (co-opted): A Three Rivers city council member who accepted cartel payouts before trying to turn on them [18, 118].

## Points of Interest / Sub-Factions
- [[The Sorcerer Zealots]]: A fanatical segment of co-opted spellcasters at the Sorcerer's Academy who worship Aelara's dark aspect [132, 146].
- [[The Excavation Corps]]: The labor forces and guards stationed at sites like the Bracamonte Mine to excavate ancient artifacts [149].

## Campaign History & Session Events
- **Session 1-3**: The party was hired to destroy a secret cargo barge chartered by the cartel in Three Rivers Harbor, resulting in the death of Xotolin Swiftclaw [118, 123].
- **Session 4**: The cartel retaliated by murdering Teagan Kearie, kidnapping his young son, and hanging city guards to seize total control of Three Rivers [122, 127].
- **Session 5-7**: Huitecoc tracked the party to the village of Cojute, summoning a magical thunderstorm that forced them to flee into the desert [131].
- **Session 8**: The party discovered that the cartel was forcing excavations at the Bracamonte Mine to locate the shattered pieces of Aelara's Ruby to resurrect her dark aspect [149, 151].
