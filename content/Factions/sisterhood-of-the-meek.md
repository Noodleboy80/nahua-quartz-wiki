# Sisterhood of the Meek

> [!abstract] Overview
> The Sisterhood of the Meek is a secretive, militant order of combat-trained "nuns" operating under a religious guise to bypass cartel lines and fight for the self-determination of the Nahuan people [112, 154]. From their hidden sanctuary beneath [[Mision de la Cruz]], they orchestrate guerrilla attacks to dismantle cartel control [112, 154].

## Description & Atmosphere
Members of the order disguise themselves as simple, peaceful acolytes of the Eternal Pantheon [113, 154]. Underneath their religious habits, however, they are highly disciplined martial artists and swordsmaids [154]. They value personal freedom and self-determination above all else, though internal tensions exist between those who favor defensive protection and those who advocate for total, unyielding warfare against their oppressors [154].

## Key Inhabitants & Associated NPCs
- [[Sor Lobos]]: The aging co-founder, a legendary former mercenary who values patience, forgiveness, and free choice [154].
- [[Sor Villareal]]: A Level 3 Tabaxi paladin who manages tactical operations with an iron fist and a binary "with us or against us" worldview [154, 155].
- [[Lilith Erevan]]: A feral tiefling shadow-monk and highly skilled assassin who was raised and trained by the order [75, 174].
- [[Sor Wanda]]: A battle-hardened sister who serves as Sor Lobos's trusted aide de camp [154].

## Points of Interest / Sub-Factions
- [[The Traditionalists / Pacifists]]: Led by Sor Lobos, focusing on harboring refugees, healing, and preserving Nahuan-Imperial religious traditions [113, 154].
- [[The Militants / Hardliners]]: Led by Sor Villareal, advocating for aggressive tactical strikes against the cartel, showing little mercy to anyone standing in their path [154].

## Campaign History & Session Events
- **Session 4**: Teagan Kearie directed the party to seek out the Sisterhood in Mont Rey, providing them with a token of alliance [126].
- **Session 7**: When the party fell into a cartel trap, Sor Lobos personally intervened to rescue them and guide them to their subterranean fortress [140, 147].
- **Session 8**: Lilith led Arcturus and Dust into the hidden sanctuary [149]. The party met with the high leadership, who tasked them with investigating cartel excavations at the Bracamonte Mine [148, 149]. Lilith faced a significant personal crossroad regarding whether to formally take [[The Oath of Eternal Devotion]] to bind her life to the order [77, 176].
