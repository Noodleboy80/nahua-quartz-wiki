# Mont Rey

> [!abstract] Overview
> Mont Rey is the second-largest city in Nahua, serving as a clean, orderly desert metropolis controlled and maintained by the Ironclaw Cartel [132, 138]. Despite the cartel's iron grip, it is a bustling hub for academic magic study, local trade, and subterranean resistance movements [132, 144].

## Description & Atmosphere
The city features two major roads that run from its outer borders toward a grand central plaza [132, 138]. It is exceptionally clean, well-kept, and free of visible street crime because the cartel maintains total de facto order [132, 138]. Cartel members and guards walk the streets openly, co-existing with students, merchants, and commoners under a veneer of peace [132, 138].

## Key Inhabitants & Associated NPCs
- [[Alondra's Brother]]: A red-haired elf geologist who relocated to Mont Rey after his daughter was killed in the border pogroms [148].
- [[Mateo]]: A deceptive central market shopkeeper who acts as a cartel double agent [133, 140].
- [[Jesus Maria "Chema" Gonzales]]: A treacherous guide who lures unsuspecting travelers into cartel traps [145].
- [[Lilith Erevan]]: A shadow monk of the Sisterhood who operates in the city's back alleys [75, 76].

## Points of Interest / Sub-Factions
- [[Mercado Central]]: The massive public market at the heart of the city, known for its diverse stalls and rare luxury services [148, 149].
- [[Sorcerer's Academy]]: A sprawling red-adobe campus featuring high arches, long glass windows, a central wizard's tower, and a massive subterranean dungeon [145, 146].
- [[Temple of the Veiled Monarch]]: A towering temple dedicated to the cartel's divine figureheads, dominating the central plaza [132, 138].

## Campaign History & Session Events
- **Session 4**: Teagan Kearie and Theodemar instructed the party to flee toward Mont Rey to seek out the Sisterhood of the Meek [126].
- **Session 5-7**: The party arrived in Mont Rey following their escape from the chupacabra caves [132, 138]. They bartered with the academy wizards to bind Arcturus's cursed intelligence-draining focus, the Mote in the Eye, which required retrieving illegal banned beverages from the academy's locked basement dungeon [132, 146].
- **Session 7**: Jesus "Chema" Gonzales betrayed the party, leading them into a narrow alleyway where they were surrounded by cartel forces [145]. Lilith Erevan ambushed the attackers, saving the party from a near-deadly trap [145].
- **Session 8**: Alondra spotted and reconnected with her red-haired geologist brother at the Mercado Central, while Lilith guided Arcturus and Dust out of the city streets toward the Sisterhood's sanctuary [148, 149].
- **Session 9**: Nyx and Dust survived a magical ferry ride, while the rest of the party infiltrated the academy's deep basement library to retrieve the book "A History of Nahuan Magic" [152, 153].
