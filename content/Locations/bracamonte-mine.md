# Bracamonte Mine

> [!abstract] Overview
> The Bracamonte Mine is a vast, open-cut mining operation outside Mont Rey that has been heavily co-opted by the Ironclaw Cartel [149]. Nominally an iron mine for forging weaponry, the cartel utilizes forced labor to excavate the site in search of shattered magical shards [149].

## Description & Atmosphere
The mine consists of a massive, multi-leveled circular pit carved deep into the desert floor [149]. The surface is dominated by a sprawling, dusty stone sorting complex where heavy iron chains, iron slag, and heavy munitions are processed [149, 151]. The air is constantly filled with choking rock dust, the clatter of pickaxes, and the harsh commands of armed cartel guards [149]. Deep beneath the operational tunnels lie unstable ancient caverns where long-forgotten magic resides [134, 150].

## Key Inhabitants & Associated NPCs
- [[Alondra's Brother]]: A red-haired elf geologist who was hired by the Bracamonte Mining Company to analyze rock compositions [148, 149].
- [[Cuacoatl]]: A gargantuan, ancient guardian snake beast that was awakened in the lower caverns by the cartel's deep digging [150, 175].

## Points of Interest / Sub-Factions
- [[The Circular Pit]]: The primary excavation site where labor forces extract raw iron and sift through stones [149].
- [[The Sifting Complex]]: A heavily guarded stone facility used to process rock fragments and isolate magical materials [149].
- [[The Deep Fissures]]: Ancient subterranean tunnels that connect the mine to wild, untamed magic caverns [134, 150].

## Campaign History & Session Events
- **Session 8**: The Sisterhood of the Meek tasked the party with conducting a stealth reconnaissance mission at the mine [148]. While exploring, Alondra discovered her brother was working there [148, 149]. The party infiltrated the deep fissures where they encountered and slew the colossal guardian snake Cuacoatl [150]. They discovered the cartel is searching for the shattered fragments of Aelara's lost ruby, which hum and resonate when brought together [149, 151].
