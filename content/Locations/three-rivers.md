# Three Rivers

> [!abstract] Overview
> Three Rivers is a high-tension, heavily segregated border city positioned between the oppressive elvish-led Imperium and the ancestral lands of Nahua [118, 127]. Home to a massive, marginalized Nahuan migrant minority, the city serves as a bustling commercial port that has recently fallen under the de facto control of the violent Ironclaw Cartel [118, 127].

## Description & Atmosphere
The city revolves around its massive harbor docks, where cargo barges from all corners of the Imperium constantly load and unload trade goods [118]. The streets are lined with old, cobblestone roads that are missing stones in some areas, surrounded by historic but slowly decaying buildings [119, 120]. The atmosphere is thick with political unrest, heavy segregation, and the constant threat of extremist pure-blood militias and cartel paramilitary forces [118, 123, 127].

## Key Inhabitants & Associated NPCs
- [[Teagan Kearie]]: City council representative for the majority-Nahuan migrant population who was murdered by the cartel [118, 122].
- [[Arcturus Erevan]]: A twinkish half-elf Nahuan diplomat who secretly organized and funded the underground resistance [118, 121].
- [[Theodemar]]: A flamboyant drow/elf noble and cat burglar who operates a lavish estate on the city's outskirts [121, 161, 162].
- [[Captain of the Guard of Three Rivers]]: The former commander of the town guard who fled into the desert with his family [127].
- [[Xotolin Swiftclaw]]: A brutal Tabaxi berserker who served as a harbor cargo guard for the cartel [121, 168].

## Points of Interest / Sub-Factions
- [[Three Rivers Harbor]]: The bustling trade harbor where cargo barges unload goods and where the cartel's secret shipment arrived [118, 123].
- [[Theodemar's Estate]]: A large, luxurious manor on the outskirts of the city surrounded by lush rolling farmland and tall fences [121, 123].
- [[Nahuan Quarter]]: The segregated enclave that housed the city's Nahuan minority before it was destroyed [123, 175].
- [[The Winking Goblin]]: A local tavern associated with the bribe lists of corrupt city officials [18, 177].

## Campaign History & Session Events
- **Session 1**: The party met in Three Rivers [118]. Teagan Kearie hired them to carry out a covert raid on a cartel-chartered ship arriving in the harbor to destroy its mysterious cargo [118].
- **Session 3**: The party executed a midnight infiltration of the docks, swimming through the harbor to board the ship, where they defeated Xotolin Swiftclaw and destroyed the shipment [123, 124].
- **Session 4**: Following the raid, the Ironclaw Cartel retaliated by assassinating Teagan Kearie and hanging city guards at the gate [122, 127]. An Imperial-backed extremist pure-blood militia subsequently burned down the Nahuan Quarter, killing hundreds of residents and forcing the survivors—including Arcturus—to flee into the desert [23, 127].
