# Mision de la Cruz

> [!abstract] Overview
> Mision de la Cruz is a ruined, desert-reclaimed abbey that secretly serves as the primary headquarters, refuge, and training ground for the militant [[Sisterhood of the Meek]] [112, 175]. Originally a peaceful sanctuary for weary travelers, its underground complex has been repurposed into a hidden fortress [112, 114].

## Description & Atmosphere
To the outside world, the abbey appears completely vacant, abandoned to the relentless desert winds with cracked, crumbling outer walls and a caved-in chapel roof [112]. Beneath this dilapidated facade lies a massive, secret subterranean complex accessed via a hidden trapdoor behind the altar [112]. The underground chambers are warm and bustling, containing living quarters, tactical maps, combat training pits, and quiet prayer rooms [112].

## Key Inhabitants & Associated NPCs
- [[Sor Juana Ines de la Cruz]]: The gentle drow cleric who founded the mission to harbor weary souls and was martyred by the cartel [113, 114].
- [[Sor Lobos]]: The legendary aging mercenary co-founder who coordinates sanctuary operations [114, 154].
- [[Sor Villareal]]: An iron-fisted Tabaxi paladin who leads the Sisterhood's combat squads [154, 155].
- [[Sor Wanda]]: A combat-capable sister appointed by Sor Lobos to serve as her primary aide de camp [154].

## Points of Interest / Sub-Factions
- [[The Subterranean Complex]]: The hidden network of barracks, armories, and training halls used to mount operations against the cartel [112].
- [[The Caved-in Chapel]]: The ruined surface chapel which hides the entrance to the fortress from patrolling cartel scouts [112].
