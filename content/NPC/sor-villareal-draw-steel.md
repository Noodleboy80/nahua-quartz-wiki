# Sor Villareal

> [!info] Quick Summary
> Sor Villareal is a fierce Tabaxi Paladin who serves as a high-ranking leader within the [[Sisterhood of the Meek]]. Clad in heavy chain mail and wielding an iron will alongside her longsword, she maintains an iron-fisted, uncompromising command of the Sisterhood's hidden operations. She acts as a tactical commander operating from their underground sanctuary beneath the desert sands.

## Narrative & Lore
- **Background & Motivations:** Sor Villareal is a studious former sage who took up the paladin's oath to stand against the oppression of the [[Ironclaw Cartel]] [7, 8]. Unlike the Sisterhood's gentle founder, Sor Lobos, Villareal is far less forgiving and open [7, 8]. She wields her authority with an "iron fist" and actively adopts a binary worldview: in the heat of battle, those who are not actively standing with the Sisterhood are treated as enemies [7, 8]. She is driven by an absolute desire to crush the cartel's extortion and violence, regardless of the cost [7, 8].
- **Key Relationships:**
  - **[[Sisterhood of the Meek]]**: Currently directs the militant operations of this undercover order of warrior-nuns [7, 8].
  - **Sor Lobos**: Serves under the legacy of the aging founder, though she rejects Lobos's open-hearted and forgiving style of leadership [7, 8].
  - **Sor Wanda**: Works in parallel with the founder's appointed aide de camp to maintain the order [7].
- **Notable Campaign Events:**
  - She oversees the training and defense of the secret sanctuary hidden directly below the caved-in chapel of [[Mision de la Cruz]] [6, 8]. She coordinates covert surveillance, defensive drills, and retaliatory strikes against cartel marauders who attempt to extort the surrounding desert villages [6, 7].

## Draw Steel Statblock

> [!statblock] Sor Villareal — Tier 1 Leader
> **Stamina:** 50 | **Stability:** 2 | **Speed:** 6 | **Size:** 1 (Medium)
> **Characteristics:** Might +2, Agility +1, Reason +1, Intuition +0, Presence +2
> 
> ### Free Strikes & Basic Actions
> - **Longsword Slash** (Melee): Distance 1, 1 creature, Might vs Defense. **Damage:** 5 slashing. The target is marked until the start of Sor Villareal's next turn; while marked, the next opportunity attack against them does not cost a reaction and deals +2 damage.
> - **Shield Bash** (Melee): Distance 1, 1 creature, Might vs Defense. **Damage:** 3 bludgeoning. The target is pushed 1 square and slowed (speed halved) until the end of their next turn.
> 
> ### Signature & Heroic Abilities
> - **Divine Smite** (Trigger: On a successful Longsword Slash): Sor Villareal expends 1 Heroic Resource to channel pure radiant power. The attack deals an additional 4 radiant damage. If the target is a fiend or undead, this additional damage increases to 6 radiant.
> - **Inquisitorial Command** (Presence vs Ward / Cost: 2 Heroic Resource): Distance 5, 1 creature, Presence vs Ward. Sor Villareal utters an absolute decree that fractures the target's resolve. On a success, the target must immediately spend their reaction to move their full speed directly away from Sor Villareal or fall prone (target's choice).
> - **Lay on Hands** (Cost: 3 Heroic Resource): Touch, 1 willing ally. Sor Villareal touches a wounded companion, expending her divine pool of energy. The target recovers 15 Stamina and is immediately cured of any one physical or mental condition currently affecting them.
