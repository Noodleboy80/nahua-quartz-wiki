# Dust

> [!info] Quick Summary
> Dust is a swift, stealthy desert-dwelling scout and rogue who harnesses the dust and wind of the Nahuan sands to blind and outmaneuver his foes. Often seen operating in tandem with Nyx, Dust serves as a lethal ambusher who can strike from the shadows before vanishing in a cloud of swirling sand.

## Narrative & Lore
- **Background & Motivations:** Raised in the dry, unforgiving Nahuan desert, Dust learned to utilize the environment to hide and survive. He joined the party in Three Rivers, finding common cause with Arcturus and Lilith in pushing back against both the elvish militias and the Ironclaw Cartel.
- **Key Relationships:**
  - **[[Nyx]]**: His close companion and fellow combatant with whom he boarded the hazardous ferry ride beneath the Sorcerer's Academy.
  - **[[Nahuan Resistance]]**: Fights alongside Arcturus, Lilith, and Gormless against cartel influence in Mont Rey.
- **Notable Campaign Events:**
  - Helped defend the party against cartel forces in the alleys of Mont Rey, fighting alongside Arcturus and Eztli.
  - Boarded the one-way river ferry with Nyx, defending the boat from a horde of ghouls and homunculi.

## Draw Steel Statblock

> [!statblock] Dust — Tier 1 Ambusher
> **Stamina:** 44 | **Stability:** 2 | **Speed:** 6 | **Size:** 1 (Medium)
> **Characteristics:** Might +1, Agility +3, Reason +1, Intuition +2, Presence +1
> 
> ### Free Strikes & Basic Actions
> - **Dual Scimitar Slash** (Melee): Distance 1, 1 creature, Agility vs Defense. **Damage:** 5 slashing. The target is disoriented (subtracts 1d4 from their next attack check) until the start of Dust's next turn.
> - **Sirocco Sand-Dart** (Ranged): Distance 10, 1 creature, Agility vs Reflex. **Damage:** 4 piercing + target must succeed on an Agility saving throw or be disoriented until the end of their next turn due to sand in their eyes.
> 
> ### Signature & Heroic Abilities
> - **Sandstorm Fade** (Free Action / Cost: 1 Heroic Resource): Dust triggers a localized burst of sand. Dust shifts up to 3 squares and can immediately make an Agility (Stealth) check to become hidden if any cover or obscurom is available.
> - **Dust Devil Strike** (Agility vs Reflex / Cost: 2 Heroic Resource): Distance 1. Dust strikes from an unexpected angle. On a success, target takes 7 slashing damage, and is blinded (cannot make ranged attacks and has disadvantage on melee attacks) until the end of their next turn.
> - **Sirocco Whirlwind** (Cost: 3 Heroic Resource): Dust spins rapidly in a flurry of scimitar strikes and swirling dust. All enemies adjacent to Dust must make an Agility saving throw. On a failure, they take 8 slashing damage, are pushed 2 squares away from Dust, and fall prone. On a success, they take 4 slashing damage and are disoriented until the end of their next turn.
