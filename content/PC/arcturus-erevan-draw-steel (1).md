# Arcturus Erevan

> [!info] Quick Summary
> Arcturus Erevan is a twinkish half-elf courtier who publicly served as a diplomatic ambassador for the nation of Nahua. Secretly, he used his scrappy childhood upbringing to organize and equip the underground [[Nahuan Resistance]] in the border city of [[Three Rivers]]. Following a spontaneous marriage to [[Lilith Erevan]], he disappeared into the desert after pure-blood militias burned down the city's [[Nahuan Quarter]].

## Narrative & Lore
- **Background & Motivations:** Born on the borders of the Imperium and Nahua, Arcturus was largely abandoned by his politically ambitious Elvish father and had a rough, scrappy upbringing in [[Three Rivers]], learning to fight dirty, read people, and talk his way out of trouble. After receiving an education in Tuacan, he was hired by the Nahuan government and sent back to Three Rivers as an ambassador to advocate for the Nahuan minority. Motivated to push back against the Imperium's oppression of Nahuans, he secretly established and armed a resistance movement underground.
- **Key Relationships:**
  - **[[Lilith Erevan]]**: His wife; they married spontaneously in Three Rivers after growing close over four months, shortly before he went missing.
  - **[[Nahuan Resistance]]**: The underground movement he helped organize, fund, and equip in [[Three Rivers]].
  - **Teagan**: A government official whom Arcturus discovered was receiving bribery payoffs from the [[Ironclaw Cartel]].
- **Notable Campaign Events:**
  - Organized and funded the Nahuan Resistance underground before his home was burned down during the horrific [[The Three Rivers Riot]].
  - Disappeared into the desert following the riot, leaving behind his wife Lilith, who assumes he is either dead or has abandoned her.
  - Acquired a cursed, mind-altering intelligence-draining orb known as the **[[Mote in the Eye]]**, which granted him visions of alternate timelines at the permanent cost of his intelligence.
