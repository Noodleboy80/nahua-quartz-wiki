# Lilith Erevan

> [!info] Quick Summary
> Lilith Erevan is a chaotic good feral tiefling Way of Shadow monk and expert assassin of the [[Sisterhood of the Meek]]. Boasting striking platinum blonde hair, light blue skin, and bat-like wings sprouting from her shoulder blades, she is a silent and deadly hunter who stalks the battlefield, striking from the shadows before vanishing.

## Narrative & Lore
- **Background & Motivations:** Born to fanatical parents of [[The Cult]], Lilith was offered as a horrific child sacrifice at a young age. After witnessing her childhood friend's sacrifice, she was rescued at age 13 by the nuns of the Sisterhood of the Meek in Mont Rey, grabbing her friend's broken silver dragon pendant as she fled. She was trained as a cold, lethal assassin by the Sisterhood on the condition that she serve their cause. However, she has always felt used and kept at arm's length by her peers, leaving her wary of organizations.
- **Key Relationships:**
  - **[[Arcturus Erevan]]**: Her husband; they grew close during her solo mission in Three Rivers and spontaneously married, though he disappeared the next day.
  - **[[Sisterhood of the Meek]]**: Her training order; she was recently presented with the choice of taking [[The Oath of Eternal Devotion]] to formally bind herself to them forever.
  - **[[The Cult]]**: The dark fanatical organization her parents belonged to, which sacrificed her closest childhood friend.
- **Notable Campaign Events:**
  - Met Arcturus in Three Rivers while on a solo mission to gather intelligence on the underground resistance, resulting in a sudden marriage the day before the Three Rivers Riot.
  - Rejoined the party in Mont Rey, serving as the "ex machina" that ambushed and saved them from cartel agents led by Jesus "Chema" Gonzales.
  - Led Arcturus and Dust back to the safety of [[Mision de la Cruz]] to meet with the leaders of the Sisterhood.

## Draw Steel Statblock

> [!statblock] Lilith Erevan — Tier 1 Ambusher
> **Stamina:** 45 | **Stability:** 2 | **Speed:** 6 (Fly 6) | **Size:** 1 (Medium)
> **Characteristics:** Might +1, Agility +5, Reason +1, Intuition +3, Presence +1
> 
> ### Free Strikes & Basic Actions
> - **Shadow Shortsword Dao** (Melee): Distance 1, 1 creature, Agility vs Defense. **Damage:** 5 piercing. The target is marked by shifting shadows until the start of Lilith's next turn. While marked, any ally gets a +1 bonus to attack checks against them.
> - **Unarmed Strike** (Melee): Distance 1, 1 creature, Agility vs Defense. **Damage:** 4 bludgeoning.
> - **Shadow Dart** (Ranged): Distance 12, 1 creature, Agility vs Reflex. **Damage:** 4 psychic.
> 
> ### Signature & Heroic Abilities
> - **Feral Agility** (Free Action): Lilith utilizes her feral heritage. Her walking and flying speed are doubled to 12 squares until the end of her turn. Once utilized, this ability cannot be used again until she spends a turn without moving.
> - **Flurry of Blows** (Bonus Action / Cost: 1 Heroic Resource): Immediately after hitting a target with Shadow Shortsword Dao, Lilith makes two Unarmed Strikes as a free action. Each strike deals 3 bludgeoning damage and pushes the target 1 square on a success.
> - **Shadow Step** (Free Action / Cost: 1 Heroic Resource): Lilith dissolves into shadows. If she is in dim light or darkness, she teleports up to her speed (6 squares) into another unoccupied square of dim light or darkness.
> - **Deflect Missiles** (Reaction / Trigger: Lilith is targeted by a ranged weapon attack / Cost: 1 Heroic Resource): Lilith catches the missile, reducing the damage from the attack by 1d10 + 9. If the damage is reduced to 0, Lilith can immediately spend her reaction to hurl the missile back at an enemy within distance 12 as a free strike (Agility vs Defense, dealing 5 damage).
> - **Vanish into Shadow** (Cost: 2 Heroic Resource): Distance 8. Lilith conjures a 15-foot radius of magical darkness on a point she can see. The area is heavily obscured, and all creatures within it other than Lilith are blinded. She can cast this without spell slots or components.
