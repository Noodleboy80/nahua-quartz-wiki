# Nyx

> [!info] Quick Summary
> Nyx is a quiet and mysterious traveler who was originally discovered inside an ancient, water-locked beach temple. Wielding strange, oceanic and extraplanar void powers, she serves as a highly adaptable and lethal solo skirmisher, currently fighting her way through a ghoul-infested underground river along with Dust.

## Narrative & Lore
- **Background & Motivations:** Found dormant within a forgotten temple on a deserted shoreline, Nyx possesses no clear memory of her origin but is deeply attuned to ancient, extraplanar forces. She is driven to discover why the cartel's excavations are destabilizing the boundaries of her home dimension.
- **Key Relationships:**
  - **[[Dust]]**: Her close companion with whom she took a dangerous river ferry ride beneath the Sorcerer's Academy.
  - **[[Nahuan Resistance]]**: Traveled across the desert with Gormless, Alondra, and Arcturus.
- **Notable Campaign Events:**
  - Enjoyed a brief rest at the Mercado Central in Mont Rey before the party raided the Sorcerer's Society campus.
  - Boarded a mysterious river ferry beneath the academy campus along with Dust, where they were attacked by a relentless swarm of ghouls and homunculi.

## Draw Steel Statblock

> [!statblock] Nyx — Tier 1 Solo
> **Stamina:** 48 | **Stability:** 2 | **Speed:** 6 | **Size:** 1 (Medium)
> **Characteristics:** Might +1, Agility +2, Reason +1, Intuition +3, Presence +2
> 
> ### Free Strikes & Basic Actions
> - **Tidal Lash** (Ranged spell): Distance 6, 1 creature, Intuition vs Reflex. **Damage:** 5 cold. The target is pulled 1 square closer to Nyx (or pushed 1 square away, Nyx's choice).
> - **Void Dagger** (Melee): Distance 1, 1 creature, Agility vs Defense. **Damage:** 4 piercing + 2 psychic.
> 
> ### Signature & Heroic Abilities
> - **Beach Temple Ward** (Reaction / Cost: 1 Heroic Resource / Trigger: Nyx is hit by an attack): Nyx conjures a spectral barrier of ancient stone and seawater. The damage from the attack is reduced by 6, and Gormless or another ally within distance 3 can shift 1 square as a free reaction.
> - **Abyssal Slip** (Free Action / Cost: 1 Heroic Resource): Nyx dissolves into a mist of cold sea-brine. She shifts up to her Speed (6 squares), moving through enemy spaces without provoking opportunity attacks, and gains a +1 bonus to Defense until the start of her next turn.
> - **Tomb of the Deep** (Intuition vs Ward / Cost: 3 Heroic Resource): Distance 8, 1 creature. Nyx conjures a sphere of crushing water around the target. On a success, the target takes 10 cold damage, is knocked prone, and is suffocating/drowning (can take no actions other than attempting a Might save to escape the tomb at the end of their turn).
