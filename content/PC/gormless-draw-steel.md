# Gormless Ironfoot

> [!info] Quick Summary
> Gormless Ironfoot is a sturdy, traveling dwarf from Nahua who became the newest charge of the itinerant preacher Hrogar Ravineheart. Clad in heavy armor and wielding a massive dwarven shield and warhammer, Gormless serves as the party's indomitable frontline protector, standing tall against the thugs of the Ironclaw Cartel.

## Narrative & Lore
- **Background & Motivations:** Originally a traveler from the ancestral lands of Nahua, Gormless met Hrogar Ravineheart and joined him as a traveling companion and protector. He was recruited by Arcturus and the party in Three Rivers shortly before the uprising, motivated to defend the oppressed Nahuan minority and help his companions survive the cartel's pursuit.
- **Key Relationships:** 
  - **[[Hrogar RavineHeart]]**: His mentor and traveling companion who rescued or guided him in his journeys.
  - **[[Nahuan Resistance]]**: Joined the fight alongside Arcturus and Lilith during their escape from Three Rivers.
- **Notable Campaign Events:**
  - Met the party in Three Rivers and stood shoulder-to-shoulder with them during the harbor docks skirmish against city guards.
  - Accompanied the party through the perilous Nahuan desert to the city of Mont Rey, where he was spotted enjoying a rare moment of luxury (mani-pedis) at the Mercado Central alongside Alondra and Nyx.

## Draw Steel Statblock

> [!statblock] Gormless Ironfoot — Tier 1 Brute
> **Stamina:** 60 | **Stability:** 3 | **Speed:** 5 | **Size:** 1 (Medium)
> **Characteristics:** Might +3, Agility -1, Reason +1, Intuition +1, Presence +1
> 
> ### Free Strikes & Basic Actions
> - **Warhammer Slam** (Melee): Distance 1, 1 creature, Might vs Defense. **Damage:** 6 bludgeoning. The target is slowed (speed halved) until the start of Gormless's next turn.
> - **Shield Shove** (Melee): Distance 1, 1 creature, Might vs Defense. **Damage:** 3 bludgeoning + Gormless pushes the target 2 squares and can immediately advance into the caved square.
> 
> ### Signature & Heroic Abilities
> - **Dwarven Resilience** (Trigger: Gormless is hit by a physical attack / Cost: 1 Heroic Resource): Gormless shrugs off the blow. The incoming attack's damage is reduced by 5, and Gormless gains +1 Stability until the end of his next turn.
> - **Ironfoot Bastion** (Cost: 2 Heroic Resource): Gormless plants his shield into the ground. All allies within distance 2 gain a +1 bonus to Defense, and any enemy that attempts to move out of an adjacent square to Gormless suffers a free strike from Gormless's Warhammer Slam.
> - **Earth Shatter** (Cost: 3 Heroic Resource / Might vs Defense): Gormless strikes the ground with force. All enemies in a 3-square cone must make a Might saving throw. On a failure, they take 8 bludgeoning damage and are knocked prone. On a success, they take 4 bludgeoning damage and are slowed until the end of their next turn.
