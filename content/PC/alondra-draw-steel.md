# Alondra

> [!info] Quick Summary
> Alondra is a brilliant elvish scout and stargazer who carries a massive brass telescope, utilizing her geology-focused intellect and starlight magic to guide her companions. She is currently separated from her companions, trapped deep inside the Mont Rey Sorcerer's Society's infinite, never-ending tower library.

## Narrative & Lore
- **Background & Motivations:** An academic-minded elf with a strong grasp of geography and earth science, Alondra is driven to unravel the magical and geological secrets of Nahua. Her brother is a geologist working for the Bracamonte Mining Company, which has co-opted local excavation sites.
- **Key Relationships:**
  - **[[Alondra's Brother]]**: Her geologist sibling working in Mont Rey, who joined the mining operations after losing his daughter in Three Rivers.
  - **[[Nahuan Resistance]]**: Traveled across the desert with Arcturus, Gormless, and the rest of the party to reach Mont Rey.
- **Notable Campaign Events:**
  - Used her telescope to spot the approaching lightning storm generated by Huitecoc, warning the party of his arrival at Cojute.
  - Celebrated a brief respite at the Mercado Central in Mont Rey with Gormless and Nyx, where she unexpectedly spotted her brother.
  - Became separated from the party during a raid on the Sorcerer's Academy library, finding herself lost in a infinite, caved-in library tower watched over by faceless librarians.

> ### Free Strikes & Basic Actions
> - **Starlight Piercer** (Ranged spell): Distance 15, 1 creature, Agility vs Reflex. **Damage:** 5 radiant. The target glows with a faint starlight beacon, preventing them from utilizing invisibility or partial cover until the start of Alondra's next turn.
> - **Geologist's Pick** (Melee): Distance 1, 1 creature, Agility vs Defense. **Damage:** 3 piercing.
> 
> ### Signature & Heroic Abilities
> - **Telescope Scan** (Free Action): Alondra scans the battlefield to find structural weaknesses. One ally within distance 10 gains a +2 bonus to their next attack check or Might save made before the start of Alondra's next turn.
> - **Guiding Beacon** (Agility vs Reflex / Cost: 1 Heroic Resource): Distance 12, 1 creature. Alondra projects a concentrated starlight beam. On a success, the target takes 6 radiant damage, and any ally who attacks this target before the start of Alondra's next turn deals +2 damage.
> - **Starlight Shroud** (Reaction / Cost: 1 Heroic Resource): Triggered when an enemy within distance 10 makes an attack check against Alondra or an ally. Alondra flashes starlight to blind them. The target of the attack gains a +2 bonus to Defense or Reflex (target's choice) against the triggering attack.
> - **Infinite Archive Ward** (Cost: 3 Heroic Resource / Reason vs Ward): Distance 6, 1 creature. Alondra summons the infinite geometry of the infinite library. On a success, the target takes 8 psychic damage and is restrained in place as shadowy, spectral book stacks trap them. They must succeed on a Reason saving throw at the end of their turn to escape the restraint.
