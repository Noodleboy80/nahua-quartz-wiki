# Master Index: Nahua Campaign

A comprehensive index of all key entities, locations, factions, events, and artifacts from the "Nahua Campaign" notes and player sheets.

## 1. Characters & NPCs

- [[Arcturus Erevan]] - A chaotic neutral half-elf courtier sorcerer (Aberrant Mind) and paladin (Oath of Conquest) who organized the underground Nahuan resistance in Three Rivers before fleeing into the desert.
- [[Lilith Erevan]] - A chaotic good feral tiefling Way of Shadow monk and assassin of the Sisterhood of the Meek who survived a childhood cult sacrifice and later briefly married Arcturus.
- [[Isabella Nightshade]] - A chaotic neutral tiefling warlock of the Pact of the Fiend who works under a charlatan background and commands an imp familiar named Lurk.
- [[Lurk]] - A small, mischievous imp familiar bound to Isabella Nightshade through her Pact of the Chain.
- [[Sir Gralden Stoneblade]] - A lawful neutral human champion fighter who serves as a loyal armored bodyguard and minion for Isabella Nightshade.
- [[Sor Juana Ines de la Cruz]] - A kind drow cleric of the Eternal Pantheon who founded the de la Cruz Mission to harbor lost souls and was brutally murdered by the Ironclaw Cartel.
- [[Sor Lobos]] - An aging human mercenary who prolongs her life past its natural end, who co-founded and led the Sisterhood of the Meek to fight cartel oppression and protect self-determination.
- [[Sor Villareal]] - A level 3 tabaxi paladin of the Sisterhood of the Meek who wields her authority with an iron fist, taking a far less forgiving stance than Sor Lobos.
- [[Sor Wanda]] - A trusted sister within the Sisterhood of the Meek appointed by Sor Lobos to serve as her combat-capable aide de camp.
- [[Teagan]] - A corrupt Nahuan government official who has received bribes from the local cartel according to captured ledger pages.
- [[Arcturus's Father]] - An ambitious elf diplomat of the Imperium who abandoned his half-elf family on the borders to secure his political career.
- [[Lilith's Friend]] - A childhood friend of Lilith who was tragically offered as a child sacrifice by Lilith's parents' cult, leaving behind a frozen silver pendant.

## 2. Locations (Settlements, Dungeons, Regions)

- [[Three Rivers]] - A tense border city between the Imperium and Nahua where Arcturus was raised, met Lilith, and organized an underground rebellion.
- [[Mont Rey]] - A desert-surrounded settlement near the de la Cruz Mission where the cartel paraded Sor Juana's body and where the Sisterhood of the Meek operates.
- [[Mision de la Cruz]] - A vacant, desert-reclaimed abbey that hides a sprawling subterranean complex used as a sanctuary and training ground by the Sisterhood of the Meek.
- [[The Imperium]] - A powerful and oppressive elvish-dominated empire bordering Nahua that exercises strict control and systematic discrimination over Nahuans.
- [[Nahua]] - A neighboring country rich in traditions and ancestry that is heavily oppressed and exploited by the political might of the Imperium.
- [[Nahuan Quarter]] - A neighborhood in Three Rivers home to the Nahuan minority that was raided and burned to the ground by an extremist pure-blood militia.

## 3. Factions & Organizations

- [[Sisterhood of the Meek]] - A militant order of combat-trained "nuns" who use their religious disguise to bypass cartel guards and fight to dismantle cartel control.
- [[Ironclaw Cartel]] - A violent and powerful organized crime syndicate that controls the desert region, extorts local towns, and actively bribes Nahuan officials.
- [[Nahuan Resistance]] - An underground partisan group in Three Rivers organized by Arcturus Erevan to fight against Imperial discrimination and militia violence.
- [[The Cult]] - A dark fanatical religious group that Lilith's parents belonged to, notorious for demanding horrific child sacrifices to prove devotion.
- [[Royal College of Nahua]] - A scholarly or government-affiliated academic institution in Nahua with which Arcturus has been associated.
- [[Sorcerer Society]] - A prominent faction of spellcasters noted within the regional lore of the campaign.
- [[Sisters Sangrientas]] - A local faction or organization operating within the regional political landscape.

## 4. Key Historical Events & Quests

- [[The Imperium Crusade]] - A major historical military crusade launched by the Imperium prior to Sor Juana Ines de la Cruz settling in the desert.
- [[The Murder of Sor Juana Ines de la Cruz]] - A tragic historical turning point where cartel bandits assassinated the beloved drow cleric and paraded her body through Mont Rey to intimidate the populace.
- [[The Three Rivers Riot]] - A devastating night of violence backed by the Imperium where an elvish pure-blood militia burned down the Nahuan Quarter, killing hundreds.
- [[Lilith's Solo Mission to Three Rivers]] - Lilith's first solo assignment from the Sisterhood of the Meek to gather intelligence on the underground Nahuan resistance, leading to her meeting Arcturus.
- [[The Marriage of Lilith and Arcturus]] - A spontaneous, alcohol-fueled marriage between Lilith and Arcturus that occurred just one day before the Three Rivers riot and Arcturus's sudden disappearance.
- [[The Oath of Eternal Devotion]] - A life-altering choice presented to Lilith after 23 years of service to formally take an oath of lifelong devotion to the Sisterhood, relinquishing personal relationships.

## 5. Significant Artifacts & Lore

- [[Mote in the Eye]] - A warm obsidian crystal orb arcane focus carried by Arcturus that grants users a vision of an alternate past but permanently drains one intelligence point.
- [[The Broken Pendant of a Silver Dragon]] - A cold-to-the-touch broken silver dragon pendant grabbed by Lilith from her sacrificed friend, representing her motivation to fight the cult.
- [[Cartel Ledger Pages]] - Incriminating administrative sheets from the "Winking Goblin" demonstrating that multiple Nahuan officials, including Teagan, accepted cartel bribes.
- [[Smashed Penny]] - A physical coin-like token from the Winking Goblin tavern kept by Arcturus as a memento in his gear.
- [[Ruby Tiara]] - A highly valuable Tiara carried in Arcturus's backpack as part of his adventuring spoils.
- [[Papers of Ambassadorship]] - Official state documents carried by Arcturus proving his diplomatic status representing the interest of Nahua in Three Rivers.
