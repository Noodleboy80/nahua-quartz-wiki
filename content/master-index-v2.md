# Master Index: Nahua Campaign (v2)

A comprehensive index of all key entities, locations, factions, events, and artifacts from the "Nahua Campaign" notes and player sheets.

## 1. Characters & NPCs

- [[Arcturus Erevan]] - A chaotic neutral half-elf courtier sorcerer (Aberrant Mind) and paladin (Oath of Conquest) who organized the underground Nahuan resistance in Three Rivers before fleeing into the desert.
- [[Lilith Erevan]] - A chaotic good feral tiefling Way of Shadow monk and assassin of the Sisterhood of the Meek who survived a childhood cult sacrifice and later briefly married Arcturus.
- [[Isabella Nightshade]] - A chaotic neutral tiefling warlock of the Pact of the Fiend who works under a charlatan background and commands an imp familiar named Lurk.
- [[Lurk]] - A small, mischievous imp familiar bound to Isabella Nightshade through her Pact of the Chain.
- [[Sir Gralden Stoneblade]] - A lawful neutral human champion fighter who serves as a loyal armored bodyguard and minion for Isabella Nightshade.
- [[Sor Juana Ines de la Cruz]] - A kind drow cleric of the Eternal Pantheon who founded the de la Cruz Mission to harbor lost souls and was brutally murdered by the Ironclaw Cartel.
- [[Sor Lobos]] - An aging human mercenary who co-founded and led the Sisterhood of the Meek to fight cartel oppression and protect self-determination, prolonging her life past its natural end.
- [[Sor Villareal]] - A level 3 tabaxi paladin of the Sisterhood of the Meek who wields her authority with an iron fist, taking a far less forgiving stance than Sor Lobos.
- [[Sor Wanda]] - A trusted sister within the Sisterhood of the Meek appointed by Sor Lobos to serve as her combat-capable aide de camp.
- [[Teagan Kearie]] - A corrupt half-elf Nahuan government official and city council representative who was assassinated by the cartel after hiring the party to destroy a cartel shipment.
- [[Arcturus's Father]] - An ambitious elf diplomat of the Imperium who abandoned his half-elf family on the borders to secure his political career.
- [[Lilith's Friend]] - A childhood friend of Lilith who was tragically offered as a child sacrifice by Lilith's parents' cult, leaving behind a frozen silver pendant.
- [[Hrogar RavineHeart]] - A bald, white-bearded Hill Dwarf cleric of the Life Domain and acolyte of the Eternal Pantheon who wandered as an itinerant preacher.
- [[Gormless Ironfoot]] - A dwarf traveler from Nahua who became Hrogar's newest charge and was recruited by the party in Three Rivers.
- [[Alondra]] - An elvish PC with a telescope and a red-haired geologist brother, who was separated from the party inside a never-ending tower library.
- [[Nyx]] - A mysterious PC who was originally found in an ancient beach temple and was later attacked by ghouls and homunculi during a ferry ride with Dust.
- [[Dust]] - A PC companion who fought along with Nyx on a river ferry against ghouls and homunculi.
- [[Eztli]] - A spellcaster PC associated with the Sorcerer's Academy who fought alongside Arcturus and Isabella during a cartel ambush.
- [[Ignacio "Nacho" Raymundo Gutierrez Castillo de la Selva]] - A brash and gallantly showboating paladin (Oath of Devotion) and former archknight of House Mazatlan who defeated the crime lord "El Ratoncillo" before mysteriously disappearing.
- [[Lord Teodoro Acatl]] - A renowned military leader and swordsman of Nahua who served as Ignacio's personal tutor in both combat and high society mannerisms.
- [[El Ratoncillo]] - A fledgling crime lord in Nahua who amassed a criminal army and was beheaded by Ignacio in pitched one-on-one combat.
- [[Josef]] - A nephew of the village elder Abel in Cojute who is secretly an immortal "Great Jaguar" guardian of the natural magic of Nahua.
- [[Abel]] - The long-time village elder of Cojute who celebrated the annual "pixca" harvest bonfire festival with Josef for nearly 60 years.
- [[Tristan]] - The young son of Teagan Kearie who was kidnapped by the cartel after his father's murder.
- [[Mateo]] - A deceptive shopkeeper in Mont Rey who acts as a cartel double agent and attempts to trick the party into a cartel ambush.
- [[Jesus Maria "Chema" Gonzales]] - A treacherous cartel agent who pretended to guide the party to the Sisterhood but instead led them directly into an alley ambush.
- [[Andre Tulio "The Serpent"]] - A wood elf druid (Circle of the Moon) and warlock (Archfey) who wanders the land as an outlander and commands a sprite familiar named Puck.
- [[Puck]] - An invisible sprite familiar bound to Andre Tulio "The Serpent".
- [[Frart]] - A traveling goblin bard with a mandolin who was mentored by Abel and Josef, and who sings ancient tales of magic in old Nahua.
- [[Alondra's Brother]] - A red-haired elf geologist working for the Bracamonte Mining Company who moved to Mont Rey after his daughter was killed in the pogroms.
- [[Captain of the Guard of Three Rivers]] - The ex-captain of the Three Rivers town guard who fled into the desert with his family, blaming the party for the cartel's de facto takeover.
- [[Cuacoatl]] - A massive guardian snake boss slain by the party in the caverns of the Bracamonte Mine.
- [[Crepusculo]] - A shadow-taloned extraplanar nightmare beast of the night sky, appointed as a guardian by the god Tzitzimitl to guard the obsidian pyramid gateway.

## 2. Locations (Settlements, Dungeons, Regions)

- [[Three Rivers]] - A tense border city between the Imperium and Nahua where Arcturus was raised, met Lilith, and organized an underground rebellion.
- [[Mont Rey]] - The second-largest city in Nahua, controlled by the Ironclaw Cartel, featuring a sorcerer's academy and the Mercado Central.
- [[Mision de la Cruz]] - A caved-in, vacant desert-reclaimed abbey that hides a sprawling subterranean sanctuary used as a staging ground by the Sisterhood of the Meek.
- [[The Imperium]] - A powerful, oppressive, elvish-dominated empire bordering Nahua that strictly discriminates against Nahuans and launched a crusade in the desert.
- [[Nahua]] - A rich ancestral country bordering the Imperium that suffers under Imperial oppression, exploitation, and cartel activity.
- [[Nahuan Quarter]] - A neighborhood in Three Rivers home to the Nahuan minority that was raided and burned to the ground by an extremist pure-blood militia.
- [[Cojute]] - A small, quiet desert village where Abel and Josef live and where the party celebrated the "pixca" harvest bonfire.
- [[Bracamonte Mine]] - A vast, multi-leveled circular pit outside Mont Rey where the cartel forces excavations to search for the fragments of Aelara's lost ruby.
- [[Tuacan]] - A distant region where Ignacio Acatl was raised, and where Arcturus received a modest education far from his father's career.
- [[The Dread Sepulcher of Harica]] - An ancient dungeon cavern hidden deep below the chupacabra caves where wild magic has transformed Nahuan wildlife.
- [[Chupacabra Cave]] - A fissure cave of impossible height formed by deep earth movements where chupacabra packs dwell.
- [[Poblo]] - A desert town or region the party set out toward when searching for the Sisterhood of the Meek.

## 3. Factions & Organizations

- [[Sisterhood of the Meek]] - A militant order of combat-trained "nuns" disguised as religious acolytes who use their cover to fight cartel oppression and protect self-determination.
- [[Ironclaw Cartel]] - A violent and powerful organized crime syndicate that controls the desert, extorts local towns, and actively bribes Nahuan officials.
- [[Nahuan Resistance]] - An underground partisan group in Three Rivers organized by Arcturus Erevan to fight against Imperial discrimination and militia violence.
- [[The Cult]] - A dark, fanatical religious group Lilith's parents belonged to, notorious for demanding horrific child sacrifices to prove devotion.
- [[Royal College of Nahua]] - An academic institution with which Arcturus was associated before working for the Nahuan government.
- [[Sorcerer Society]] - A sprawling campus of red adobe in Mont Rey co-opted by the cartel to study magic, divided between co-opted students and fanatical cultists.
- [[Sisters Sangrientas]] - A local faction or organization operating within the regional political landscape of the desert.
- [[House Mazatlan]] - One of the last great noble houses of Nahua that Acatl and Ignacio supported before the Imperium's invasion.
- [[Nightshade Syndicate]] - A criminal group whose members clashed with the Ironclaws during a desert deal gone wrong.
- [[Order of the Great Jaguars]] - An ancient order of six mortal guardians co-founded by the gods and imbued with immortality and natural magic to protect Nahua's magic.

## 4. Key Historical Events & Quests

- [[The Imperium Crusade]] - A major historical military crusade launched by the Imperium prior to Sor Juana Ines de la Cruz settling in the desert.
- [[The Murder of Sor Juana Ines de la Cruz]] - A tragic historical turning point where cartel bandits assassinated the beloved drow cleric and paraded her body through Mont Rey to intimidate the populace.
- [[The Three Rivers Riot]] - A devastating night of violence backed by the Imperium where an elvish pure-blood militia burned down the Nahuan Quarter, killing hundreds.
- [[Lilith's Solo Mission to Three Rivers]] - Lilith's first solo assignment from the Sisterhood of the Meek to gather intelligence on the underground Nahuan resistance, leading to her meeting Arcturus.
- [[The Marriage of Lilith and Arcturus]] - A spontaneous, alcohol-fueled marriage between Lilith and Arcturus that occurred just one day before the Three Rivers riot and Arcturus's sudden disappearance.
- [[The Oath of Eternal Devotion]] - A life-altering choice presented to Lilith after 23 years of service to formally take an oath of lifelong devotion to the Sisterhood, relinquishing personal relationships.
- [[The Battle of El Ratoncillo]] - Ignacio's famous victory over crime lord "El Ratoncillo" at House Mazatlan's holding, culminating in the crime lord's public beheading.
- [[The Pixca Celebration]] - An annual harvest bonfire festival in Cojute where villagers weave wreaths and Josef secretly burns magical herbs to bless crops.

## 5. Significant Artifacts & Lore

- [[Mote in the Eye]] - A cursed warm obsidian crystal orb focus that grants visions of alternate realities but permanently drains one intelligence point.
- [[The Broken Pendant of a Silver Dragon]] - A cold-to-the-touch broken silver dragon pendant grabbed by Lilith from her sacrificed friend, representing her motivation to fight the cult.
- [[Cartel Ledger Pages]] - Incriminating administrative sheets from the "Winking Goblin" demonstrating that multiple Nahuan officials, including Teagan, accepted cartel bribes.
- [[Aelara's Ruby]] - A sacred magical ruby shattered into resonating fragments that the cartel is digging for in the Bracamonte Mine.
- [[Moving Map]] - A magical map room requiring a constant magical power source that allows users to will specific rooms into existence.
- [[A History of Nahuan Magic]] - A secret historical book hidden in the basement library of the Mont Rey Sorcerer's Society.
- [[Nahuan 4Loko]] - A banned beverage in Mont Rey that the academy wizards wanted for a basement rager, requiring a dungeon retrieval.
- [[Smashed Penny]] - A physical coin-like token from the Winking Goblin tavern kept by Arcturus as a memento in his gear.
- [[Ruby Tiara]] - A highly valuable tiara carried in Arcturus's backpack as part of his adventuring spoils.
- [[Papers of Ambassadorship]] - Official state documents carried by Arcturus proving his diplomatic status representing the interest of Nahua in Three Rivers.
- [[Screaming Chain]] - A mystical chain weapon wielded by Huitecoc that allows him to channel *Shocking Grasp* spell effects at long distances.
